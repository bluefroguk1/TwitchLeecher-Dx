## Summary

A dark brown theme with a red accent and turquoise highlights, selectable
under Settings > Theme. Defines every theme colour, including the named
colours from the theme colours PR, and fixes tab labels and the progress
bar so they stay readable on dark themes.

## Depends on

the theme colours PR

Until those are merged this PR also shows their changes; please review the last commit only.

## Testing

Tested on Windows: switching themes, readable tab labels and progress bar.
